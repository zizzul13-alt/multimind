## Selected collision/differentiation logic

Piña vs Washi: woven strand interlacement vs dispersed/formed fibrous sheet.

Batik vs Adire: both resist processes, but accepted scope and process sequence must stay lineage-specific rather than becoming generic dyed cloth.

Jamdani vs Kente/Yakan: in-weave supplementary/discontinuous figure behavior vs narrow-band assembly or other loom-structure behavior.

Kente: `strand → narrow band → stitched macro-sheet`; seamless full-width cloth fails the construction identity.

Masi/Siapo/Ngatu/Baganda: all share beaten-fibre-sheet physics but must remain differentiated by evidenced sheet joining, treatment and process branches rather than copied motifs.

Damascene: host/insert mechanics rather than dark-metal/gold-line appearance.

Dry stone: contact/fit/gravity without mortar rather than decorative stone surfacing.

Urushi: layered coating/cure/polish process rather than shine alone.

Murano mezza filigrana: fused transparent/glass strand construction rather than generic glass gloss.
