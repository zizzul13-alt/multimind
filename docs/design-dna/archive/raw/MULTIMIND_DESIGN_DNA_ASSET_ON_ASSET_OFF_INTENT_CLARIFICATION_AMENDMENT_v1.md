# MULTIMIND DESIGN DNA --- ASSET-ON / ASSET-OFF INTENT CLARIFICATION AMENDMENT v1

ROLE: DESIGN-DNA RESEARCH BRANCH REPORTS TO: DESIGN DNA HQ / PROJECT
GOVERNOR STATUS: GOVERNANCE CLARIFICATION --- CUMULATIVE / NO RESET

# 0. PURPOSE

This amendment corrects ambiguity in the interpretation of the existing
Asset-Off Identity / Asset-Off Survival law.

THIS IS NOT A RESET.

DO NOT discard, reopen, or weaken valid prior research concerning
mechanism-first Design DNA, material construction physics, deterministic
projection, named-reference differentiation, lineage, Marriage Engine
behavior, fallback behavior, accessibility, provenance, licensing, or
EQ2/EQ3 results already valid under existing governance.

The correction concerns only the intended relationship between:

`DESIGN DNA ↔ VISUAL ASSETS ↔ ASSET-OFF SURVIVAL`

# 1. AUTHORITATIVE INTENT

`ASSET-OFF SURVIVAL IS A RESILIENCE REQUIREMENT.`

`IT IS NOT AN ASSET-MINIMIZATION REQUIREMENT.`

Design DNA is NOT required to avoid textures, images, patterns,
ornaments, illustrations, photographs, or other visual assets.

Where legally eligible and compositionally appropriate, rich visual
assets MAY be used extensively to increase fidelity, specificity,
atmosphere, material expression, cultural/reference identity, and
presentation quality.

A Design DNA implementation SHOULD NOT deliberately reduce useful
eligible assets merely because identity must also survive without them.

`ASSET-OFF CAPABLE != ASSET-AVOIDANT`

`MECHANISM-FIRST != ASSET-FORBIDDEN`

# 2. THREE-STATE FIDELITY MODEL

## FULL / ASSET-ON

All eligible assets required or desired by the projection are available.

The implementation may use textures, photographs, illustrations,
patterns, ornaments, material imagery, masks, SVG/vector resources,
decorative imagery, environmental imagery, reference-specific visual
resources, and other legally eligible visual assets.

This may be the highest-fidelity expression of the DNA.

Asset-on presentation MAY be substantially richer than asset-off
presentation.

There is NO requirement that asset-on and asset-off have equal visual
richness.

## DEGRADED / PARTIAL-ASSET

One or more assets are unavailable, loading, blocked, unsupported,
intentionally disabled, excluded by licensing, removed by
accessibility/performance policy, or otherwise unable to participate.

The system must degrade gracefully.

Remaining mechanisms and eligible assets must continue expressing the
intended DNA.

A missing asset MUST NOT cause uncontrolled collapse into generic UI.

## ASSET-OFF / STRUCTURAL SURVIVAL

No nonessential visual assets are assumed available.

The DNA must remain recognizably itself through sufficient deterministic
mechanisms such as form, geometry, composition, spacing, hierarchy,
typography, color behavior, borders, construction logic, material
behavior, layering, lighting behavior, interaction, motion where
available, semantic structure, and other applicable mechanisms.

Asset-off tests:

`IDENTITY RESILIENCE`

It does NOT define the maximum richness allowed in normal operation.

# 3. MATERIAL CLARIFICATION

Existing law remains valid:

`MATERIAL = CONSTRUCTION PHYSICS / MECHANISM, NOT MERELY TEXTURE`

It MUST NOT be interpreted as:

`MATERIAL MUST NOT USE TEXTURE OR IMAGE ASSETS`

Correct interpretation:

`MATERIAL IDENTITY = construction / behavior mechanism + eligible asset enrichment where useful`

For example, paper/fibrous material may derive structural identity from
layering, edge behavior, fiber logic, translucency, surface response,
construction, wear, and color/light interaction, while ALSO using
eligible paper textures, fiber imagery, masks, patterns, scans, or
photographic resources.

If those assets fail, the construction/mechanism layer prevents collapse
into a generic surface.

# 4. ASSET FAILURE LAW

Expected runtime behavior:

`ASSET AVAILABLE → maximum intended fidelity`

`ASSET LOADING → structural/mechanistic DNA already renders → asset progressively enriches`

`ASSET PARTIALLY FAILED → graceful degradation → remaining projection compensates where possible`

`ASSET COMPLETELY UNAVAILABLE → structural/mechanistic identity survives → DNA remains recognizable`

Forbidden:

`asset missing → generic white/black UI → DNA identity disappears`

# 5. FLUID THEME-SWITCHING INTENT

All Design DNA output is ultimately intended for runtime use inside the
MultiMind web application.

Theme changes MUST NOT destroy unrelated application state, including
conversation state, multi-agent responses, debate progress, user input,
uploaded context, session identity, or other application-domain state.

Preferred progressive behavior:

`THEME SELECTED → deterministic structural projection available → UI transitions → eligible assets load/enrich → FULL presentation reached`

A theme SHOULD NOT require its complete asset corpus to finish loading
before recognizable presentation begins.

# 6. SHARED ASSETS

Different Design DNA references MAY share eligible assets where
appropriate, including material textures, noise/grain resources, masks,
structural patterns, lighting resources, generic construction assets,
and other primitives.

`SHARED ASSET != SHARED IDENTITY`

`SHARED ENGINE FAMILY != DUPLICATE NAMED REFERENCE`

Asset reuse must not erase reference-specific identity.

# 7. ASSET QUANTITY

There is NO governance requirement to artificially minimize asset
quantity.

If a DNA legitimately benefits from many eligible assets, it may use
many assets.

Research branches must not reject useful assets solely because another
mechanism approximates the effect, asset-off identity already passes, or
the reference can technically survive without them.

Relevant questions are: 1. Does the asset materially improve fidelity or
expression? 2. Is it compatible with the reference/DNA? 3. Is provenance
known? 4. Is it `PRODUCTION_WEB_ELIGIBLE` for production use? 5. Is
there a valid degradation/fallback path? 6. Can runtime delivery remain
acceptable?

Asset quantity itself is NOT a failure condition.

# 8. LICENSING / PROVENANCE

Research evidence and production eligibility are distinct.

An asset useful for research/reference evidence is NOT automatically
eligible for production deployment.

Production eligibility is governed by the separate Production-Web Asset
Eligibility Amendment.

`PERSONAL_USE_ONLY != PRODUCTION_WEB_ELIGIBLE`

`REFERENCE_ONLY != PRODUCTION_WEB_ELIGIBLE`

`UNKNOWN_OR_UNCLEAR_RIGHTS != PRODUCTION_WEB_ELIGIBLE`

If an asset cannot legally participate in production:

`production asset excluded → fallback/degraded projection activates → DNA identity survives`

# 9. RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE

Branches MUST NOT optimize toward an unnecessarily asset-poor Design DNA
system.

Where relevant, researchers MAY identify useful visual assets, asset
classes, reusable/shared assets, reference-specific assets, provenance,
licensing constraints, asset-on projection behavior, degraded behavior,
and asset-off fallback behavior.

Do not fabricate asset requirements merely to increase asset count.

Mechanism evidence and asset evidence remain distinct evidence classes.

# 10. PRIOR-WORK RECONCILIATION

DO NOT automatically reopen previously settled targets.

For prior contracts using terms such as asset-off, asset-independent,
mechanism-first, texture-independent, or structural identity, classify:

`A — ALIGNED` Asset-off means resilience. No correction required.

`B — AMBIGUOUS` Language could encourage asset minimization or leaves
maximum-fidelity asset-on behavior underspecified. Clarify during Global
Calibration unless a deeper contradiction exists.

`C — MISINTERPRETED` Prior contract explicitly/effectively forbids
useful eligible asset enrichment without another valid reason. Targeted
remediation required; preserve valid mechanism research.

A separate license state may coexist:

`D — LICENSE_BLOCKED` A desired/candidate asset is not production-web
eligible. Exclude/replace the asset; do not invalidate the DNA
mechanism.

A/B/C are intent classifications. D is an asset eligibility condition
and may coexist with them.

# 11. NON-NEGOTIABLE LAW

Carry forward:

`ASSET-OFF SURVIVAL LAW`

Asset-off survival is a resilience requirement, not an
asset-minimization requirement.

Eligible assets may be used extensively where they materially improve
fidelity, identity, material expression, atmosphere, or presentation
quality.

Full-fidelity asset-on presentation may be substantially richer than
asset-off presentation.

However, failure, absence, delayed loading, licensing exclusion, or
intentional disabling of nonessential assets must not collapse the
recognizable structural/mechanistic identity of the DNA.

`ASSET-ON → maximum eligible fidelity`

`PARTIAL / DEGRADED → graceful degradation`

`ASSET-OFF → recognizable DNA survives`

# 12. GOVERNANCE

NO RESET. NO GLOBAL REOPEN. NO AUTOMATIC INVALIDATION OF PRIOR EQ2/EQ3.
NO PRODUCTION MODIFICATION. NO CANON LOCK BY BRANCH.

Apply cumulatively to all subsequent Design DNA research.
