2. HOLD SEMANTICS CHANGE
======================================================================

HOLD is no longer a valid terminal completion disposition for an eligible
in-scope research target.

HOLD means:

    TEMPORARY BLOCKED / RESEARCH CONTINUES.

A HOLD may document:
- inaccessible evidence,
- weak provenance,
- unresolved contradiction,
- license/access limitation,
- unclear mechanism,
- insufficient formal observation,
- missing adaptation proof,
- implementer-independence gap.

But it does NOT close the target.

The target remains in the active residual queue until it reaches PASS.

======================================================================

"License prevents direct asset reuse" is NOT an eligibility exception if the
mechanism can still be researched and translated asset-free.
