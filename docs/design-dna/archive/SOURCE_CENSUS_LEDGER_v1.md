This ledger tracks discovered Design-DNA Markdown families and their current archival disposition.

It is intentionally separate from `RAW_SOURCE_MANIFEST_v2.md`:

- `RAW_SOURCE_MANIFEST_v2.md` tracks raw files already mirrored/verified plus known high-priority debt.

No count in this document is a final historical denominator until census exhaustion is declared.
